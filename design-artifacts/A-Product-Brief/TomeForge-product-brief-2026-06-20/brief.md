# TomeForge Product Brief

## 1. Product Vision

TomeForge aims to provide the best overall experience for creating, managing, and printing D&D cards — with an easy workflow and no difficulties for the best looking card. Our promise: *"The easiest way to turn your D&D content into beautiful, printable cards — organized your way, no paper wasted."*

The vision is to provide a highly intuitive and user-friendly platform that empowers both players and DMs to organize their cards by character, class, campaign, or any custom category, minimizing paper waste during printing. While initially focusing on D&D Spell cards, the long-term goal is to expand to other D&D elements like capacities, items, characters, monsters, and NPC statblocks, evolving into a comprehensive toolkit for D&D enthusiasts.

## 2. Target Audience

TomeForge serves two primary audiences equally:

**Players** — who want their character's spells, capacities, and items organized into beautiful, printable decks. They value ease of use, efficient printing, and the flexibility to customize their gaming experience.

**Dungeon Masters** — who want NPC statblocks, monster cards, merchant inventories, and campaign resources at their fingertips. They need a tool that scales across multiple characters and sessions.

Both audiences share common needs:
*   Are tired of manual card creation or disorganized existing methods.
*   Want to create custom cards for homebrew content or unique character builds.
*   Appreciate digital tools that enhance their tabletop experience.
*   Prefer physical cards during gameplay but want a streamlined creation and printing process.

## 3. Problem Statement

Currently, D&D players and DMs have very few options for creating custom cards, and the existing tools produce poor-looking results. The end product rarely does justice to the effort players put into their characters and campaigns. This leads to:

*   **Poor visual quality:** Existing tools produce cards that look unprofessional and lack visual appeal — players deserve cards they can be proud of.
*   **Lack of beautiful art/visuals:** There is no easy way to incorporate lovely artwork into custom cards, making them feel generic and uninspired.
*   **Limited options:** Only a handful of tools exist for card creation, offering little choice in formats, styles, or content types.
*   **Time-consuming manual processes:** Players spend significant time writing out or digitally formatting cards, often with disappointing visual results.
*   **Wasted resources:** Inefficient printing layouts lead to excessive paper use.

TomeForge's core mission is to change this — making every card visually stunning so players and DMs can take pride in their homemade creations.

## 4. Solution Overview

TomeForge will address these problems by providing a centralized web application with the following core functionalities:

*   **Stunning Visuals:** High-quality card rendering with built-in visual themes and templates that make every card look polished and professional.
*   **Artwork Integration:** Ability to add custom art and illustrations to cards, making creations feel unique and personal.
*   **Intuitive Card Creation:** A user-friendly interface for inputting spell details and generating cards with ease.
*   **Flexible Organization:** Tools to create and manage decks, allowing organization by character, class, campaign, or custom categories.
*   **Optimized Printing:** Efficient layout options for printing cards in standard Poker/TCG and Tarot sizes, minimizing paper waste.
*   **Customization Options:** Support for various D&D editions and the ability to incorporate homebrew rules and content.
*   **Expandable Content:** Initial focus on spell cards, with a roadmap to include capacities, items, characters, and monsters.

## 5. Key Features

### Core Features (MVP)

*   **Spell Card Creator:** Form to input spell name, level, school, casting time, range, components, duration, description, and higher-level effects.
*   **Deck Management:** Create, rename, delete, and organize decks of spell cards.
*   **Print Layout Editor:** Select card size (Poker/TCG, Tarot), arrange multiple cards per page, and export to PDF for printing.
*   **Artwork Integration:** Upload and apply custom art/illustrations to cards — making each card feel unique and personal.
*   **Visual Themes:** Pre-designed templates ensuring every card looks polished and professional out of the box.
*   **Public Sharing (Free):** Share deck lists publicly via links — great for showing creations on Reddit, Discord, or social media. This serves as organic marketing for TomeForge.

### Monetization Model

**Free tier:**
*   Core spell card creator, deck management, basic printing.
*   Standard visual themes and templates.
*   Basic artwork upload functionality.
*   Public sharing — create shareable links to showcase decks (helps spread the word).

**Premium tier (subscription, e.g. $3-5/month or $30-50/year):**
*   **Expanded Content Types:** Creation tools for capacities, items, characters, and monsters.
*   **Premium Art & Themes:** Exclusive card backs, premium visual themes, and artist-designed templates.
*   **Advanced Organization:** Enhanced tagging, search, and filtering for large collections.
*   **Import/Export:** Import from external sources, export in various formats and resolutions.
*   **User Accounts:** Persistent cloud storage across devices.
*   **Ad-free experience.**
*   **High-resolution exports:** Print-quality assets for professional card printing services.

**Why subscription over one-time purchases:**
*   Sustained revenue for ongoing updates, new templates, and D&D edition support.
*   Lower barrier to entry — users can try premium for a month without a large commitment.
*   Aligns with SaaS expectations for web tools.

## 6. Success Metrics

*   **User Engagement:** Monthly active users, session duration, and frequency of card creation/printing.
*   **User Retention:** Percentage of users who return to the platform over time.
*   **Feature Adoption:** Usage rates of core features like card creation, deck management, and printing.
*   **User Satisfaction:** Net Promoter Score (NPS) and direct feedback from user surveys or forums.
*   **Monetization (Future):** Conversion rate for premium features, revenue generated from paid content.

## 7. Assumptions & Constraints

### Assumptions

*   D&D players are actively seeking tools to streamline card creation and management.
*   There is a market for both free basic functionality and premium features.
*   Users are comfortable with a web-based application for this purpose.
*   The platform can effectively handle various D&D rulesets and custom content without overwhelming complexity.

### Constraints

*   **Initial Scope:** Focus on D&D spell cards for MVP to ensure a polished core experience.
*   **Technical Resources:** Limited development team requiring prioritization of features.
*   **Performance:** The application must be performant even with a large number of custom cards.
*   **Copyright/Licensing:** TomeForge is a tool — users type in their own content. No licensed content is stored or provided for auto-completion, so users are responsible for what they enter.

## 9. Future Considerations

*   **Community Features:** Share custom cards/decks with other users, public repositories.
*   **Mobile Responsiveness:** Optimize for tablet and mobile devices for on-the-go access.
*   **AI-Powered Suggestions:** Generate spell ideas or balance checks.
*   **Localization:** Support for multiple languages.
*   **Integration with VTTs:** Direct integration with Virtual Tabletop platforms like Roll20 or Foundry VTT.
