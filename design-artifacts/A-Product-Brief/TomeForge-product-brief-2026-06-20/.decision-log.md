# Decision Log

## Session: 2026-06-20 — Product Brief Creation

### Decisions Made

1. **Product Promise**
   - *"The easiest way to turn your D&D content into beautiful, printable cards — organized your way, no paper wasted."*

2. **Dual Audience**
   - TomeForge serves both **Players** and **Dungeon Masters** equally.

3. **Core Differentiator**
   - **Visual quality** is the primary differentiator — beautiful, professional-looking cards with artwork integration. Existing tools produce poor-looking cards; TomeForge makes cards players can be proud of.

4. **Public Sharing**
   - Shared deck links are **free** (organic marketing via Reddit, Discord, social media) — not behind a paywall.

5. **Monetization Model**
   - **Freemium subscription model** — generous free tier (core creation, printing, artwork, visual themes, public sharing) + premium subscription ($3-5/month or $30-50/year) for expanded content types, premium art, advanced organization, cloud sync, and high-res exports.

6. **Artwork Integration**
   - Available as soon as possible (part of MVP). May become partially premium later if needed.

7. **User Accounts**
   - **Postponed** — not needed for MVP. Local storage first, cloud accounts later (premium feature).

8. **Copyright/Licensing**
   - TomeForge is a tool — users type their own content. No licensed content is stored or auto-completed. Users are responsible for what they enter.

9. **Initial MVP Scope**
   - D&D Spell cards only. Expanded content types (capacities, items, monsters, NPCs) are premium/roadmap.
